    Your VeriPresenX Handbook

    Everything you need to know — in about two minutes. Pick your lane below.

    🎓 I'm a Student 👔 I'm a Course Rep
    For Students: Checking In
    1Join & show up. Your Rep shares a class code — join once, then simply walk into the lecture hall for every session.
    2Watch the screen. The Rep shows a live 30-second PIN (or a rotating QR code). You must be inside the lecture geofence to check in.
    3Type it (or scan it). Enter the code on your phone — or point your camera at the QR. Your device is verified, your location is verified, your code is verified — all in one second.
    4See the ✅. You're on the live roster instantly. No paper, no queue, no "did he see my name?"
    5Track your semester. The analytics tab shows your attendance %, your excused days 🛡️, and exactly how many classes stand between you and the 70% exam threshold.

    💡 GPS or network glitching? After 3 silent failed attempts, a hidden "Request Manual Verification" button appears. Send it, raise your hand — your Rep approves you with a glance. No one ever sees the failure counter; it exists only to stop spammers.

    For Course Reps: Run a Lecture in 60 Seconds
    1Pin your location. Pick a saved hall or tap "Set Current Location" once. That becomes the invisible wall students must be inside.
    2Pick an attendance mode. Full combo (PIN + location + device), PIN + Device only (no GPS), Live GPS anchor, or QR projector mode for big halls.
    3Hit "Generate PIN." The class reads it off your screen or scans your projector QR. Watch the roster fill live.
    4Optional headcount. Tap "Conduct Physical Presence Check" and type the number of bodies you see. The app flags the difference — no ghost check-ins.
    5Approve & close. One-tap approve the manual requests you can physically see, then "Close Class & Save". Records archive forever — CSV export anytime.

    🚩 Empty seat linked to a check-in? You can't delete a student mid-session (that's the law). Tap Flag Absent instead — it instantly alerts the student, and the incident is logged permanently.

    The Invisible Shield — Why You Can Trust the Numbers
    ⏱️ Rotating PIN — the code dies every 10s, so a WhatsApp screenshot is already expired by the time it lands.
    🔒 Device Lock — the first phone you use is bound to your matric forever. One phone, one student.
    📍 Geofence — check-ins only count inside the hall area. No hostel check-ins, no "I'm on my way."
    🤝 Fail-Safe override — real problems (dead GPS, dropped network) get a human rescue hatch. Never a dead end.
    🚩 Anti-beef system — no silent deletions; every removal, flag, and approval is permanent and visible.
    👁️ Watchdog — screenshot attempts and app-switching mid-session are logged for the Rep to review.
    When Things Go Wrong
    ❌ "Invalid PIN" → the code just rotated. Ask for the current one — it's a feature, not a bug.
    ❌ "GPS too weak" → move near a window. The radius already absorbs indoor bounce, so don't fight it.
    ❌ "Device Locked" → that phone is registered to another matric. Use your own device — borrowing phones is exactly what we're blocking.
    ❌ "No GPS at all?" → your Rep can run the session in PIN + Device Lock mode. No location needed.
    ❌ Flagged absent by mistake? → see your Rep immediately. Decisions are final but never silent, and always on record.
